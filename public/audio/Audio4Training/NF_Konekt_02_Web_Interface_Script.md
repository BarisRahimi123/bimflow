# Newforma Konekt — Part 2: The Web Interface & Reporting
## Dashboard, Issues, Filtering, Multi-Edit & Reports — Training Script for ElevenLabs

### [INTRO — Friendly, Welcoming Tone]

Welcome back! This is part two of our Newforma Konekt series.

In part one, we got you set up — account, invitation, and the integration installed. Today is the workhorse lesson: actually using the web interface. This is where you'll spend most of your time, so we'll go slow and clear.

By the end, you'll know how to navigate the dashboard, work the issues list, filter like a pro, edit lots of issues at once, and pull a clean report. Let's dive in.

---

### [SECTION 1 — The Dashboard]

Let's start with where you land when you log in.

When you sign in, you usually open on the "Hub" page. Think of a hub as the top-level container. You pick the correct hub, then pick your project, and that drops you onto the project Dashboard.

The Dashboard is your at-a-glance view. It shows recent activity and stats tied to you — what's been happening, what's open, what needs attention. It's a great place to start your day, but the real work happens on the next tab.

---

### [SECTION 2 — The Issues Tab]

Here's the heart of the whole tool — the Issues tab. This is where every coordination issue lives, and where you'll filter, view, edit, and update them.

Click any issue, and it opens in a side panel on the right so you can read the details without losing your list.

One small but useful tip: the issue list is paginated, and by default it only shows 25 issues at a time. If you've got a big project and you want to see more, you can save a default filter set to show 50, 100, or even all of them. We'll talk about saving filters in just a second.

---

### [SECTION 3 — Filtering]

Now, filtering. This is the skill that turns a giant pile of issues into exactly the ones you care about.

The basic move is three steps. One — click the Filter button to open the filter panel. Two — enter the criteria you want, like a status, a zone, or who it's assigned to. Three — click Apply. Done. Your list now shows only matching issues.

But here's the real time-saver: Quick Filters. Once you've applied a filter you like, a blue "Save as quick filter" link appears next to the filter icon. Click it, give your filter a clear, descriptive name, and decide whether it's Personal — just for you — or Shared, so your whole team can use it. Then save.

Now that filter is one click away, anytime — on the web, AND inside the Navisworks integration.

One thing to remember: if you create a new Quick Filter and you're working in the Navisworks plug-in, you need to refresh your filters in there for the new one to show up. If it's missing, that refresh is usually the fix.

---

### [SECTION 4 — Viewing and Editing an Issue]

Okay, you've found your issue. Let's work on it.

When you click an issue, it expands in that side panel. In there you'll find a few things. There's a Comments tab, where the full conversation history lives and where you add your own comments. There's an Attachments tab, where you can view or add files. And there's a little pencil icon — that pencil opens "Quick edit," the fast way to change a field.

If you need the full picture, click the three dots and choose "Open Details Page." That opens the issue on its own full page, with everything laid out. To change a field there, click the pencil, make your edit, and hit Save. Simple as that.

---

### [SECTION 5 — Multi-Edit: The Big Time-Saver]

Now let me show you the feature that'll save you the most time — Multi-Edit.

Multi-Edit lets you change a bunch of issues all at once instead of one by one. Say you need to close ten issues, or push the due date on a whole batch — this is how you do it in seconds.

Here's the workflow. First, filter your list down so you're only looking at the issues you want to change — this makes selecting them way easier. Then check the boxes next to the ones you want. Click the pencil icon to open Multi-Edit. Type in the new values — the new status, the new due date, whatever you're changing. And click Apply.

That's it. Every selected issue updates at once. Pre-filtering first is the secret here — it keeps you from accidentally editing something you didn't mean to.

---

### [SECTION 6 — Reports]

Last big topic — reports. This is how you share issue information with people, in a clean P-D-F.

You can export a report and either email it as a link or print it as a P-D-F. And here's a nice touch: when someone opens the linked version, the issue titles are live hyperlinks — click a title and it jumps straight to that issue ticket. Great for meetings.

There are two ways to start a report. One — print from a filtered issue list, which carries your selection into the report setup. Two — use a saved report template stored in the Reports section.

When you configure the report, you choose what to include. You can turn issue fields on or off. And one coaching tip straight from the guide: turn the Metrics off for a cleaner, simpler report. Those charts can clutter things up if you just need the issue list.

You can save your setup as a template so you don't rebuild it every time. Then you either print it — saving as P-D-F when you do — or you schedule it. The Schedule Report option is handy: it emails the report automatically to multiple people, on a one-time or repeating basis.

Spend a little time clicking around and it'll feel natural quickly. In the next lesson, we'll cover something short but important — how to name your issues so they're crystal clear to everyone. See you there.
