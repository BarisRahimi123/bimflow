### [INTRO — Friendly, Welcoming Tone]

Hey there, and welcome! This is the first lesson in our series on Newforma Konekt — the tool we use to manage coordination issues on this project.

Quick note on the name. You'll hear it called Newforma Konekt, but you might also see it called BIM Track in older screens — same software, just a new name.

This series has four parts. Part one — that's today — is all about getting you set up: your account, your invitation, and installing the tools that connect Konekt to your modeling software. Part two covers using the web interface day to day. Part three teaches you how to name your issues. And part four covers the design issue and question workflow.

So let's start at the very beginning — getting you in the door.

---

### [SECTION 1 — What Newforma Konekt Is]

First, the big picture. What is this tool actually for?

Newforma Konekt is cloud-based coordination software. Its whole job is to help everyone on the project share, track, and talk about model coordination issues — things like clashes, conflicts, and questions.

And here's the part that makes it powerful: you can work with those issues two ways. One, from any web browser. And two, right inside your own 3D modeling software — like Navisworks or your authoring program. Same issues, same data, two doors into it.

So whether you live in the model all day or you just need to check status from a browser, Konekt keeps everyone on the same page.

---

### [SECTION 2 — Requesting Your Account]

Alright, step one for you — getting an account.

This part is simple. You send an email to the Hoffman contact listed in the document, and they'll send back an access form. On that form, you fill in the information for every single team member who needs access.

So gather your team first. If five people need in, all five go on that form. Do this early — accounts don't appear instantly, and you don't want anyone stuck on day one unable to log in.

---

### [SECTION 3 — The Invitation Email]

Once your account is requested, the real setup begins with an invitation email.

You'll get an email inviting you to join the project — or as Konekt calls it, the "hub." Inside that email is an "Activate Account" button. Click it.

That link is your gateway. It walks you through setting up your profile, getting into the web app, and connecting Konekt to Navisworks and your authoring software. So keep an eye on your inbox for that invite — and if you don't see it, check your junk folder before assuming something went wrong.

---

### [SECTION 4 — Setting Up Your Profile the RIGHT Way]

Now, this section is small but really important. When you activate your account, it asks you to create a password and fill in your details. Easy enough — but there's a naming rule here you have to follow.

In the First Name field, you need to put your company's three-letter author code in square brackets, BEFORE your first name.

So for example, if your code is H-C-C and your name is Alex, you type: open bracket, H-C-C, close bracket, space, Alex.

And listen carefully to this one: make sure there's a space between the closing bracket and the first letter of your name. That space matters.

There's one more piece. The person who is the main point of contact for your company gets a star symbol added in. So the contact would look like: bracket H-C-C, close bracket, star, Alex.

Why do we bother with all this? Because it sorts everyone neatly by company and instantly shows who the lead contact is. When there are dozens of people in the system, that little code saves everyone a ton of time. So get it right the first time.

---

### [SECTION 5 — Installing the Integrations]

Last piece for today — connecting Konekt to your software. This is what lets you see and edit issues right inside your model.

Here's the path. Log in to Newforma Konekt, and on the sidebar, click "Integrations." On that page, click "View Add-ins." Then click the program you use to download its add-in.

One handy thing to know: all the Autodesk apps use the same installer file. So you download one M-S-I file, and it covers your Autodesk programs.

Once it downloads, open that M-S-I file and follow the install wizard to the end. When it asks, install at the USER level — that's the recommended setting.

And one honest heads-up: on a lot of company computers, regular users don't have permission to install software. If the installer blocks you, that's normal — you'll just need your I-T team to help finish it. Don't fight it; loop them in.
